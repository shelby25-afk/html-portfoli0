The top 3 MOVIES

According to our taste

1. Interstellar

Interstellar is a great movie because it blends **powerful human emotion with real scientific ideas about space, time, and relativity**. The story isn’t just about exploring the universe—it’s about **love, sacrifice, and the bond between a parent and child**. The film also stands out for its **stunning visuals, immersive music by Hans Zimmer, and thought-provoking themes** that make viewers reflect on humanity’s future and our place in the universe.

2. Don't Look Up

Don't Look Up is a satirical comedy-drama that critiques modern society's response to existential threats. The film follows two astronomers who attempt to warn humanity about a comet on a collision course with Earth, but their efforts are met with skepticism and dismissal from political leaders and the media. The movie uses dark humor to highlight issues of misinformation, political apathy, and the failure of institutions to address urgent problems.

3. Leave the World Behind

Leave the World Behind is a psychological thriller that explores the consequences of a global crisis. The film follows a family who becomes trapped in their suburban home when a mysterious event disrupts the world outside. As they struggle to survive and uncover the truth behind the crisis, they must confront their own fears and the dark secrets of their community.
